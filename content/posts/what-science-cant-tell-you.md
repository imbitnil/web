---
title: "What science can't tell you"
date: "2026-02-19"
excerpt: "The scientific method is extraordinary at answering 'how.' It was never built to answer 'why should I care.'"
cover: ""
---

Science is remarkably good at telling us how the universe works. It is silent, by design, on what any of it *means*, or what we ought to value.

That's not a flaw in science — it's a boundary. Confusing the two leads to bad philosophy dressed up as physics, and bad physics dressed up as philosophy.
