Put photos for your blog posts in this folder.

Then reference them in a post's Markdown like this:

    ![description of photo](/images/posts/your-photo.jpg)

Or set it as the post's cover image in the frontmatter:

    ---
    title: "My Post"
    date: "2026-08-10"
    excerpt: "..."
    cover: "/images/posts/your-photo.jpg"
    ---

Tip: keep filenames lowercase with hyphens, no spaces (e.g. sunset-beach.jpg).
